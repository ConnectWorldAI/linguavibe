# Apple Live Translation Research Notes

## How It Works
- Built into iOS 26 (released Sept 2025) as part of Apple Intelligence
- Works in Phone calls, FaceTime, Messages, and with AirPods for in-person conversations
- Runs ENTIRELY on-device — no cloud processing, no data sent to Apple
- Both sides hear spoken translation: "As you speak, the other person hears a spoken translation of what you say, and their response is also translated"
- On-screen transcription also available during calls
- Must download language packs first (one-time download)
- Alerts both parties when translation starts ("Start Translation" notifies both sides)

## Languages Supported (as of iOS 26.5, May 2026)
- Launch (iOS 26.0): English (UK, US), French (France), German, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Spain) — **only 5 languages**
- iOS 26.1 (Nov 2025): Added Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Italian
- Total: ~9-10 language variants as of now

## Cost & Data
- FREE — included with iOS 26, no subscription
- On-device processing = no data usage during translation
- Requires Apple Intelligence compatible device (iPhone 15 Pro+, iPhone 16+, iPhone 17+)
- NOT available on older iPhones (iPhone 14 and below)

## Key Limitations (from Boostlingo testing)
1. **Overlapping audio problem**: Both sides hear TWO audio streams — the original speaker AND the AI translation simultaneously. Hard to follow.
2. **Accuracy issues**: Mistranslated "No, estoy bien" (No, I'm fine) as "I'm not fine" — meaning completely flipped
3. **Vocabulary gaps**: Failed to recognize common verb "arremanguar" (to roll up sleeve)
4. **Only ~10 languages**: vs Google Translate's 130+
5. **No dialect support**: Only "Spanish (Spain)" — no Mexican, Dominican, Colombian, etc.
6. **No learning component**: Pure translation utility, no educational value
7. **Requires newer hardware**: Only works on Apple Intelligence-capable devices
8. **Beta status**: Still labeled as beta feature
9. **EU restrictions**: Blocked for EU users with EU Apple accounts

## How the Other Side Hears It
- On phone calls: When you tap "Start Translation", the call switches to speaker mode
- Your voice is captured, translated, and played as synthesized speech to the other person
- The other person hears the AI-generated translation (not your actual voice)
- Both sides also see on-screen transcription
- The other person does NOT need an iPhone — it works on regular phone calls

## ConnectWorld AI Advantages Over Apple
1. **40+ languages with dialect support** (Dominican Spanish, Mexican Spanish, etc.)
2. **Learning-focused**: Not just translating — teaching pronunciation, vocabulary, grammar
3. **Hume AI emotional intelligence**: Understands tone, emotion, context — Apple doesn't
4. **Music-based learning**: Karaoke, song library, voice cloning — unique approach
5. **Cross-platform**: Works on any device, not just latest iPhones
6. **Conversation practice with AI**: Simulated real conversations for learning
7. **Spaced repetition**: Vocabulary retention system
8. **Progress tracking**: XP, streaks, report cards
9. **Community features**: Language exchange matching, partner messaging
10. **Offline learning**: Downloaded lessons, not just translation packs
