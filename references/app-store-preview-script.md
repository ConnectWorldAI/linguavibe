## Production Notes

**Transitions:** Use iOS-native cross-dissolve (0.3 s) between scenes. No flashy effects — keep it premium and clean.

**Pacing:** Each scene should feel snappy. The user's finger (or cursor on web capture) should already be mid-action as the scene begins — never show idle screens.

**Capture method:** Record via Xcode Simulator (iPhone 15 Pro Max frame) or physical device with QuickTime mirroring. Trim with Final Cut Pro or ScreenFlow.

**Audio guidelines:**
- Background track fades in at 0:00, peaks at 0:13, fades out at 0:28.
- No voiceover — text overlays carry the narrative.
- Subtle UI sound effects (tap, success chime) mixed at 20% volume.

**App Store requirements:**
- Must be captured from the actual app (no mockups or rendered animations).
- Maximum file size: 500 MB.
- Codec: H.264 or HEVC.
- No iPhone bezel in the video (App Store adds device frame automatically).
