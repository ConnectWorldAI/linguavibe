## Three Core Teaching Secrets (Unique Methods)

### Secret 1: Alphabet Mastery First
- Learn to properly pronounce EVERY letter of the Spanish alphabet like a native
- Foundation-first approach — don't rush to vocab/grammar before mastering sounds
- If you can say every letter perfectly, you can pronounce every word perfectly
- Schools and apps skip this, rushing to vocab and conjugations
- This is the ROOT of native-sounding pronunciation

### Secret 2: Rhythmic Reinforcement Training (RRT)
- **Proprietary method** — unique to Rocky
- Four-step process:
  1. Take relevant conversational Spanish words and phrases
  2. Learn and master their proper pronunciation
  3. Hear them spoken properly at normal speed
  4. Hear them sped up to native speed (with letter cutting, word merging)
- Addresses the #1 gap: students who know grammar/vocab but can't understand native speakers
- Trains ears to expect correct Spanish sounds vs English sounds
- Progressive speed training: normal → fast → native speed with contractions
- Example: "¿Qué es lo que está pasando con esto?" spoken at native speed sounds completely different

### Secret 3: Artificial Immersion
- Can't move to a Spanish country? Create immersion at home
- Active learning (not passive): watch Netflix in Spanish WITHOUT English subtitles
- Six-step Netflix exercise:
  1. Grab pen and paper
  2. Listen to a sentence without looking at subtitles
  3. Write down what you hear (spelling doesn't matter)
  4. Keep rewinding until you figure it out or can't
  5. Slowly say what you wrote, imitating the speaker
  6. Now check subtitles and match English to Spanish words
- Find Spanish-speaking friends, bars, barbershops, salons
- Listen to Spanish music actively
