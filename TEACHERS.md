# Fluence — AI Teacher Roster & Cultural Design System

## Design Philosophy

Every teacher in Fluence is designed to feel like a real person from a real place. They are attractive, culturally authentic, and reflect the actual demographics of the city/region they represent. Users don't just pick "a Spanish teacher" — they pick a specific person with a backstory, personality, accent, and teaching style that matches where that language is actually spoken.

---

## Teacher Design Principles

1. **Attractive** — Teachers are good-looking, well-groomed, approachable. Think "the cool teacher everyone wanted in school."
2. **Culturally authentic** — Their appearance, clothing, accessories, and style match their region's real demographics.
3. **Diverse** — Every race, ethnicity, body type, and age range represented authentically based on where they're "from."
4. **Personality-driven** — Each teacher has a distinct vibe: patient, energetic, funny, strict, chill, motivating.
5. **City-specific** — Not just "from Mexico" but "from Guadalajara" with specific local knowledge.

## Teacher Selection UX

### How Users Choose Their Teacher

```
1. Select target language → "Spanish"
2. Select region/country → "Dominican Republic"  
3. Select city (optional) → "Santo Domingo"
4. Browse available teachers → See Yamilet, Carlos, Rosalía
5. View teacher profile:
   - Avatar (realistic, attractive)
   - Voice sample (30-sec audio clip)
   - Personality description
   - Specialties (slang, business, music, etc.)
   - Teaching style (patient, energetic, strict, chill)
   - Student ratings
6. Start trial conversation (free 2-min preview)
7. Choose your teacher → Relationship begins
```

### Teacher Relationship Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Memory | Teacher remembers all past conversations and your weak points |
| Mood adaptation | Teacher adjusts energy based on time of day and your mood |
| Progress tracking | Teacher celebrates milestones, pushes when you plateau |
| Cultural tips | Teacher shares cultural context naturally in conversation |
| Song recommendations | Teacher suggests songs based on vocabulary you're learning |
| Homework | Teacher assigns practice between sessions |
| Multiple teachers | Users can have different teachers for different contexts (formal vs. casual) |

## Avatar Generation Strategy

### Phase 1 (Launch): AI-Generated Static + Voice
- Use D-ID/HeyGen to create realistic talking-head avatars
- ElevenLabs for unique voice per teacher
- Pre-rendered expressions (happy, thinking, encouraging, correcting)

### Phase 2 (Growth): Real-Time Animated Avatars
- Simli or custom pipeline for real-time lip-sync
- Facial expressions react to conversation context
- Hand gestures and body language

### Phase 3 (Scale): Full-Body Interactive Avatars
- Full-body avatars in environment (café, classroom, street)
- Location-appropriate backgrounds (Havana street, Tokyo café, Paris bistro)
- Multiple camera angles during conversation

## Cultural Authenticity Guidelines

When designing each teacher:

1. **Research real demographics** — What do people actually look like in that city? What's the racial/ethnic makeup?
2. **Clothing/style accuracy** — What do attractive young people in that city actually wear?
3. **Speech patterns** — Record real native speakers from that exact city for voice training
4. **Cultural references** — Each teacher references local music, food, sports, celebrities, memes
5. **Avoid stereotypes** — Represent diversity WITHIN each country (not all Colombians look the same)
6. **Age range** — Most teachers 23-33 (relatable to target demographic) with some older "professor" types
7. **Gender balance** — Roughly 50/50 with representation across the spectrum
8. **Attractiveness** — All teachers are good-looking but in culturally appropriate ways (not one beauty standard)
