## Background

Rickie is a Black American self-taught Spanish speaker who deliberately developed a Dominican accent after achieving fluency. He started learning Spanish at age 12 and reached fluency within 2 years. He's been teaching for 15+ years and has built a community of 900+ active students.

He holds a degree from Southern Illinois University, Edwardsville. His day job was as an Optician but he transitioned to full-time Spanish education content creation.

## Teaching Style

- **Dialect-focused**: Specializes in Caribbean Spanish (Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, Cuba) — teaches slang, colloquial phrases, and pronunciation patterns specific to each region
- **Accent coaching**: Offers "Spanish Accent Consultations" to help learners sound more natural
- **Real-world immersion**: Uses scenarios, challenges (e.g., "No English for 24 Hours"), and cultural context
- **Self-study methodology**: Teaches how to self-study effectively, with tips on retention and practice habits
- **Slang & colloquial focus**: Extensive content on regional slang that isn't taught in schools
- **Audio/video submissions**: Students submit audio and video recordings for feedback
- **AI integration**: Uses AI for conversation practice in his student community
- **Daily prompts**: Provides daily real-life prompts for practice

## Content Format

- Short-form reels showing dialect differences between countries
- "Rate My Spanish" challenge videos
- Full immersion challenge videos (24 hours no English)
- Blog posts with detailed slang guides by country/region
- Live lessons and consultations via booking platform
- Student testimonials and progress showcases

## Key Differentiators (Ideas for ConnectMe AI)

1. **Dialect-specific learning paths**: Not just "Spanish" but Dominican, Mexican, Puerto Rican, Cuban, etc.
2. **Accent coaching methodology**: Teaching HOW to develop a specific accent, not just vocabulary
3. **Slang databases by region**: Curated lists of colloquial expressions per country
4. **Audio submission & feedback loops**: Students record themselves, get accent feedback
5. **Cultural context integration**: Slang taught with cultural/historical background
6. **Immersion challenges**: Gamified "no English" challenges with accountability
7. **Self-study framework**: Structured approach to independent learning with retention tips
