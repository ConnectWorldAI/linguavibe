# "-ER Verbs" — Spanish Learning Song

## Concept:
Teach English speakers how to conjugate Spanish -ER verbs (comer, beber, correr, aprender, leer) 
through a catchy Funketón track. Each verse focuses on a different verb, 
cycling through yo/tú/él/nosotros/ustedes conjugations.

## Verbs Selected:
1. **Comer** (to eat) — como, comes, come, comemos, comen
2. **Beber** (to drink) — bebo, bebes, bebe, bebemos, beben
3. **Correr** (to run) — corro, corres, corre, corremos, corren
4. **Aprender** (to learn) — aprendo, aprendes, aprende, aprendemos, aprenden
5. **Leer** (to read) — leo, lees, lee, leemos, leen
