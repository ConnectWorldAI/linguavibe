## The Method (Visual Association / Image-Based Learning)
The CIA method he's describing is **visual association** — pairing images with vocabulary words instead of just text translations. You look at a picture that represents the word, and your brain creates a stronger memory connection than just reading "perro = dog."

This is backed by the "Dual Coding Theory" — when you encode information both visually AND verbally, retention doubles.

## How ConnectWorld AI Implements This in Curriculum

### 1. Image-Based Vocabulary Cards
- Every vocabulary word gets a vivid, culturally relevant image
- Not stock photos — AI-generated scenes showing the word in context
- Example: "mercado" (market) → colorful Dominican street market scene with fruits, vendors, people

### 2. Scene-Based Learning (Immersive Visuals)
- Full-scene illustrations where multiple vocabulary words are labeled
- Like a "Where's Waldo" but for vocabulary
- Tap any object in the scene to hear pronunciation + see the word

### 3. Visual Story Sequences
- Comic-strip style panels telling a story in target language
- Each panel introduces new vocabulary through visual context
- User reads the story, taps words they don't know

### 4. AI-Generated Cultural Scenes
- Use AI image generation (Midjourney/DALL-E style) to create:
  - Street scenes from different countries (Dominican Republic, Colombia, Spain)
  - Restaurant scenes with labeled menu items
  - Home scenes with household vocabulary
  - Market scenes with food/clothing vocabulary

### 5. "Spot the Word" Game Mode
- Show an image, user must identify which Spanish word matches
- Progressive difficulty: single objects → complex scenes → abstract concepts

## Content Ideas for the App
