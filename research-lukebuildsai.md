## His "Jarvis" System — Key Architecture
Luke built a personal AI assistant called "Jarvis" that:
1. **Monitors his app metrics** — downloads, revenue, MRR (reads from analytics APIs)
2. **Builds apps from prompts** — "POV: Jarvis is building you a $30k MRR app"
3. **Generates content** — creates social media posts, tracks engagement
4. **Manages operations** — email triage, customer support, community management
5. **Competitive research** — tracks competitor pricing, launches, reviews automatically
6. **Business intelligence** — generates daily/weekly reports, surfaces trends, flags anomalies

## Azaris.ai Platform — His Company
Azaris deploys AI agents for businesses:
- **Social Media & Content**: Monitor Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, X for brand mentions. Draft content, track engagement, generate performance reports on autopilot.
- **Email Management**: Triage emails, draft context-aware responses, escalate important ones.
- **Business Intelligence**: Pull data from tools, generate daily/weekly reports automatically.
- **Customer Support**: Resolve tickets, answer FAQs, escalate complex issues across every channel.
- **Community Management**: Keep Discord, Slack, Telegram engaged 24/7.
- **Competitive Research**: Track competitor pricing, product launches, customer reviews.

## His Workflow Architecture Pattern
1. **Tell the system what you need** (natural language goals)
2. **Agent configures itself** (connects to tools, learns brand voice, trains on data)
3. **Runs autonomously** (human-in-the-loop for review/override)

## What ConnectWorld AI Can Learn From This
1. **AI Operations Agent** — Build an internal "Jarvis" for ConnectWorld AI that monitors user metrics, content performance, and generates reports
2. **Automated Content Pipeline** — Auto-generate social media content (song translations, cultural facts, language tips) and schedule posts
3. **Multi-Channel Community Management** — AI agent monitors and responds across all social channels
4. **Competitive Intelligence** — Auto-track what Apple Translate, T-Mobile, Duolingo are shipping
5. **Voice-First Interface** — His Jarvis uses voice commands; our app already has Hume voice — we could add a "Hey ConnectWorld" assistant mode
6. **Autonomous Content Generation** — Instead of manually creating lessons/songs, have an AI pipeline that auto-generates content based on trending topics, user demand, and curriculum gaps
