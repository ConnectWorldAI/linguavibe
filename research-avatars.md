# AI Avatar Platform Research - May 2026

## Key Findings

### For Fluence, we need TWO different avatar capabilities:
1. **Real-time conversational avatars** (for AI teacher voice/video calls) - MOST IMPORTANT
2. **Pre-rendered video avatars** (for Watch & Learn content, Fluence TV) - Secondary

### Platform Comparison for REAL-TIME Conversational Use

| Platform | Latency | Pricing | Realism | API-First | Best For |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| Tavus (Phoenix-4) | <500ms | $59/mo starter, startup grant: 15K min free | Best emotional intelligence | Yes | Real-time AI agents |
| Simli | <300ms | $0.05/min (~$3/hr) | Good lip-sync | Yes | Budget real-time avatars |
| D-ID (V4 Agents) | Sub-second | $4.70/mo lite, $16/mo pro, $108/mo advanced | Good | Yes | Streaming avatars |
| HeyGen (LiveAvatar) | Moderate | $1-4/min via API | Best overall quality | Yes | Pre-rendered + some live |

### Platform Comparison for PRE-RENDERED Video Content

| Platform | Pricing | Avatar Count | Realism Score | Languages | Best For |
|----------|---------|-------------|---------------|-----------|----------|
| Synthesia | $18-67/mo | 150+ | 88% | 140+ | Corporate, training |
| HeyGen | $24-30/mo | 120+ | 85% | 175+ | Marketing, translation |
| D-ID | $4.70-108/mo | Limited (photo-based) | Good | 119 | Developer integrations |

### Key Metrics
- Synthesia lip-sync accuracy: 94%
- HeyGen lip-sync accuracy: 91%
- Tavus latency: <500ms end-to-end
- Simli latency: <300ms
- HeyGen API: $1/min standard, $4/min Avatar IV
- Simli: $0.05/min (cheapest real-time)
- Tavus startup grant: 15,000 free minutes for 3 months
