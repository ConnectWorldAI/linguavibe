## User's Feature Request
The user wants ConnectWorld AI to enable:
1. **Song translation in same key/melody** — Take any song, translate lyrics to target language, keep the musical arrangement
2. **Voice training/cloning** — User trains AI on their voice, AI sings the translated version in their voice
3. **In-app studio mode** — User can sing the translated version themselves (recording studio)
4. **AI voice swap** — Put a different AI voice on the translated song (different singer voice)

## Key Technical Requirements
- Vocal isolation (already have this)
- Lyrics extraction + translation (already have this)
- Melody/key preservation during translation (syllable matching, rhythm adaptation)
- Voice cloning (train on user's voice samples)
- In-app recording studio (record over instrumental)
- AI voice synthesis (sing translated lyrics in AI voice)
- Same key/melody constraint (pitch-matched translation)
