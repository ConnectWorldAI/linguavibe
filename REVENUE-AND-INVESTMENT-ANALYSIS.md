## Why the Jobs Section Changes Everything

**Without Jobs:**
- ConnectWorld AI = another language app competing with Duolingo (valued at $7B)
- Revenue ceiling: subscription + credits only
- User churn: people stop once they feel "fluent enough"

**With Jobs:**
- ConnectWorld AI = LinkedIn for multilingual professionals
- Revenue ceiling: dramatically higher (recruitment is a $500B global industry)
- User retention: people STAY because the platform connects them to income
- Employer lock-in: companies need certified bilingual workers, they come to us
- Certification value: our cert means something because it leads to employment

**The pitch to investors changes from:**
> "We teach languages better than Duolingo"

**To:**
> "We're the only platform where you learn a language, get certified, and get hired — all in one place. We monetize the entire journey from student to professional."
