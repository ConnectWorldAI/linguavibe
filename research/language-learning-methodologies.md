## 5 Core Methodologies Extracted

### 1. Comprehensible Input (Krashen's Theory)
- **Origin**: Stephen Krashen's research (1980s)
- **Used by**: LingQ, Dreaming Spanish, most polyglots
- **Core principle**: Listen/read content slightly above your level (i+1), understand 70-90%, learn the rest from context
- **How children learn**: This mimics natural first-language acquisition
- **Daily allocation**: 60 min listening + 30 min reading
- **App integration**: AI teacher adjusts content difficulty automatically, video/podcast library at graded levels

### 2. FSI Method (Foreign Service Institute)
- **Origin**: U.S. Department of State training program
- **Used by**: Diplomats, military linguists, intelligence officers
- **Core principle**: Structured drills → core vocabulary → sentence patterns → immediate speaking → daily use
- **Time estimates**: Spanish ~600-750 hours, Arabic/Chinese ~2200+ hours to professional fluency
- **Language difficulty tiers**: Category I (Spanish, French) = 24-30 weeks, Category IV (Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean) = 88 weeks
- **App integration**: Structured lesson progression with drill-based exercises, placement testing, time-to-fluency estimates

### 3. Massive Listening (Polyglot Method)
- **Origin**: Elite polyglots (Steve Kaufmann, Luca Lampariello)
- **Used by**: Interpreters, translators, UN staff
- **Core principle**: 70% listening, 20% speaking, 10% grammar study
- **Content types**: YouTube videos, podcasts, audiobooks, TV shows, music
- **App integration**: Audio-first content delivery, podcast integration, music translation feature

### 4. Sentence Mining
- **Origin**: Language learning community best practice
- **Used by**: Advanced learners, Anki users, polyglots
- **Core principle**: Learn words in full sentences, not isolation. "Voy a comer ahora" not "Comer = To Eat"
- **Why it works**: Brain remembers context much better than isolated vocabulary
- **App integration**: AI automatically creates flashcards from songs, videos, articles, conversations

### 5. Spaced Repetition System (SRS)
- **Origin**: Hermann Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve research
- **Used by**: Anki, Memrise, medical students, polyglots
- **Core principle**: Cards appear right before you forget them, reducing study time dramatically
- **App integration**: Already built into LinguaVibe's SRS engine with gamification

## Additional Methods Discovered

### 6. Bidirectional Translation (Luca Lampariello)
- Translate FROM target language AND back INTO target language
- Trains brain to think directly in target language
- Eliminates "foreigner pause" (mental translation delay)

### 7. Speak from Day 1 (Benny Lewis)
- Don't wait until "ready" — start speaking immediately
- Use language hacking techniques to have conversations early
- Build confidence through real interaction, not perfection

### 8. Immersive Content Consumption (LingQ Method)
- Import any content (Netflix, YouTube, podcasts, books) as lessons
- Highlight unknown words, build vocabulary in context
- Track progress with word count metrics (known words = proficiency)

## Optimal Daily Learning Formula (Research-Based)

| Time | Activity | Method |
|------|----------|--------|
| 30 min | Vocabulary review (SRS) | Spaced Repetition |
| 60 min | Listening (videos, podcasts, music) | Comprehensible Input + Massive Listening |
| 30 min | Reading (graded content, news) | Comprehensible Input |
| 30 min | Speaking (AI conversation, practice) | FSI Method + Speak from Day 1 |
| 15 min | Writing (journal entries) | Sentence Mining + Bidirectional Translation |
| **2.5 hrs** | **Total daily** | **Combined approach** |
