## Content Format Ideas for ConnectWorld AI:

### Replicable Format:
- **One phrase per reel** with AI-generated scene (Kling AI / fal.ai)
- **Consistent character designs** across all posts (brand identity)
- **Our Suno songs** as background music (not licensed tracks)
- **"Listen on ConnectWorld AI"** CTA replacing "Listen on Langbeats"
- **Comment hooks** to drive engagement + app downloads

### How We Do It Better:
1. We generate the SONGS ourselves (Suno) — they license from artists
2. We can make the characters SPEAK the phrase (HeyGen avatars)
3. We can make it INTERACTIVE in-app (tap to hear, swipe for next)
4. Our songs teach FULL conjugations, not just single phrases
5. We chain phrases into themed LESSONS (not random)
6. We add SLANG variants (Dominican vs Colombian vs Mexican version of same phrase)
7. We can generate VIDEO not just static illustrations (Kling AI)

### Production Pipeline:
1. Pick theme (transportation, feelings, street talk, etc.)
2. Write 5-10 phrases for that theme
3. Generate Suno song incorporating all phrases
4. Generate Kling AI scene for each phrase
5. Overlay text (English + Spanish)
6. Add "Listen on ConnectWorld AI" branding
7. Auto-post to TikTok/IG/YouTube via our pipeline
8. Link back to full lesson in app
